A native confirm() without a dialog handler freezes the tab for CDP

2026-08-20 · Puppeteer#dialog#confirm#cdp

Symptom

After clicking a bulk DELETE every CDP command to the tab hung (even Page.enable), puppeteer.connect did not answer, and No dialog is showing appeared after killing the process.

Cause

confirm('Are you sure you want to delete...') blocks the renderer; without page.on('dialog') nobody closes it.

Rule

Attach page.on('dialog') before the click in any such script; cure a frozen tab with browser-level Target.closeTarget plus Target.createTarget, not by restarting the bridge. The deletion has no further confirmation in the UI, so take a backup beforehand.
