ImapFlow uidValidity is a BigInt; JSON.stringify throws, systemd still 'active'

2026-08-24 · Email (IMAP/SMTP)#imapflow#json#systemd#selftest

Symptom

The inbox daemon was deployed, the unit was active, 30 selftest checks were green on both machines. The daemon parsed no mail: it was crash-looping a fraction of a second after opening the mailbox, and systemctl is-active kept answering active because the process came up between crashes.

Cause

ImapFlow returns mailbox.uidValidity as a BigInt, and JSON.stringify throws TypeError: Do not know how to serialize a BigInt. Saving state is the first thing the daemon does on a new mailbox. The fixtures could not contain that value: they were hand-written, and the type came from the library. Second layer: active in systemd means the process is alive, not that the work is being done.

Rule

Convert everything that enters state from an external library to your own type at the boundary (Number(...), String(...)). After deployment look at the daemon's own log and an artifact of its work (state file, ledger line, chat message), not the unit status: the first live run is a separate acceptance step.
