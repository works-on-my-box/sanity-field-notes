A stop script's cleanup outside the per-daemon branch killed the other daemon

2026-08-21 · Shell#daemons#pkill#testing

Symptom

stop.sh listener also stopped the monitor, which silently stopped polling.

Cause

At the end of the script a 'leftover' cleanup via pkill -f monitor.js and pkill -f tg-listener.js sat outside the loop over the selected daemon and always ran.

Rule

In daemon control scripts every operation (kill, pkill, PID-file removal) lives inside the branch of the specific daemon; after editing, verify that stopping one does not touch the other (ps before and after).
