An irreversible action in a foreign UI goes last and is verified by outcome

2026-08-24 · Web forms#forms#commit#verification#automation

Symptom

Exploring a bidding widget: a number typed into the field means nothing until Set bid is pressed (Bid: 0 with 18 in the field). After pressing, the bid becomes part of the form and cannot be taken back: clearing the field and pressing again does not reset it.

Cause

In a foreign interface state lives elsewhere than where you type: the field is a draft, the commit is a separate event, and undo may not exist. The habit 'set it, check, redo if needed' comes from your own code where rollback is free.

Rule

Put an action that cannot be undone last in the chain, after every check that could cancel it, and verify by the observable outcome (what the form shows, what the submit button says), not by what you typed. Outcome differs from intent means do not submit at all.
