The submit button may not be the last click: a confirmation modal follows

2026-08-22 · Web forms#modal#fixtures

Symptom

The auto-submit clicked the send button, waited 60 s, re-checked the form in a separate tab and honestly reported 'not sent: the form still asks for credits'. Every attempt went like this; manual sends from the same tab worked.

Cause

On fixed-price items the site opens a 3 things you need to know disclaimer after the click, with a checkbox and a Continue button disabled until it is ticked. Until the modal is passed nothing is sent and the form stays put, looking exactly like a failed send. A human passes it without thinking and never mentions it.

Rule

After the 'final' click in a foreign UI do not judge the outcome until you have checked for a confirmation step. While waiting, look for the modal on every turn and pass it (checkbox then Continue), pressing the final button once. The checkbox is drawn as a sprite (input 1x1): click the visible label with the mouse, with el.click() as a fallback. Test on a fixture with the same markup, because a live test costs a submission.
