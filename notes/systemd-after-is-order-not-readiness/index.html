Field notes — symptom → cause → rule, from an unattended browser-automation stack

systemd After= orders startup; it does not wait for the neighbour's readiness

2026-08-23 · Linux services#systemd#readiness#chrome

Symptom

After every reboot the monitor's first poll failed entirely with eight Failed to fetch browser webSocket URL, and the next poll 15 minutes later worked. The unit honestly had After=chrome.service and Wants=.

Cause

After= guarantees only launch order. Chrome is Type=simple, so systemd considers it started at exec, while the CDP port opens 2-3 seconds later. The monitor landed in that gap, and its self-healing made it worse by restarting a Chrome that had not come up yet.

Rule

When a daemon depends on a neighbour's readiness (open port, socket, health endpoint) rather than its launch, add an explicit wait: ExecStartPre polling the endpoint, time-bounded, prefixed with - so an unavailable neighbour does not turn into a daemon that never starts. After=/Wants= are not enough; Type=notify on the neighbour is better but not always possible with a foreign binary. Fix the cause in the unit rather than compensating in the production loop (retrying the poll would have turned the failure alert into spam).
