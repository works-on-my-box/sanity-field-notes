SMTP `250 OK` means accepted by your relay, not delivered; check for a bounce

2026-09-24 · Email (IMAP/SMTP)#imap

Symptom

A submission was recorded as sent on the basis of 250 2.0.0 OK. In the same minute a Delivery Status Notification (Failure) arrived: the recipient address was a Google Group closed to external senders. The inbox classifier filed the bounce as a foreign message and no alert was raised; it was found by eye in the log a session later.

Cause

The address was taken from an FAQ where it was a form caption, not a mailbox. No post-send delivery check existed: 250 OK means accepted by our SMTP server, not delivered. The classifier had no class for non-delivery reports.

Rule

Count an email as delivered only after checking the mailbox for a bounce (about 15 minutes later; a bounce class now raises an alert). Take addresses from the contact/form page, not an FAQ. In the ledger call an unverified send 'accepted by SMTP', not 'submitted'.
