spawnSync with encoding:'utf8' corrupts multibyte characters on chunk boundaries

2026-08-21 · Node.js#child-process

Symptom

A draft and a chat header contained a Cyrillic word with a replacement character in the middle, while the JSON from the child CLI was valid.

Cause

spawnSync with encoding decodes the stream in chunks; a multibyte UTF-8 character that falls on a buffer boundary is corrupted.

Rule

Launch child processes without encoding and decode the whole buffer at once: (r.stdout || Buffer.alloc(0)).toString('utf8'). Applies to every wrapper around child scripts.
