A stage that silently drops candidates makes any audit impossible

2026-08-24 · Linux services#logging

Symptom

Asked 'did we wrongly skip anything overnight', the logs could not answer: 8 of 15 new items vanished before the drafter without a single line. Each cause had to be reconstructed by re-running the filter function over the saved JSONL.

Cause

The monitor had const why = cheapFilter(j); if (why) continue;: decision made, nothing recorded. Every other stage logged its reason; the cheapest and most frequent one did not, precisely because it looked like 'just a filter'.

Rule

Every stage that rejects a candidate must leave a line 'what was rejected and why', including the cheapest and most obvious. Test while writing: could I answer next week why a specific item did not reach the end without running anything? If the answer is 'I would re-run the code over the data', the record does not exist.
