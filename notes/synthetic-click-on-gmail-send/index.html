A synthetic .click() on Gmail's Send button sends nothing and leaves no trace

2026-08-23 · Chrome & DevTools Protocol#gmail#click#trusted-events#cdp#verification

Symptom

Two canary emails were 'sent' over CDP: element.click() on the element labelled Send ran without errors and the script reported clicked. The messages were in neither Inbox, Sent nor Drafts; a search with in:anywhere found nothing.

Cause

Gmail's button is a div[role=button] that reacts to a real sequence of mouse events; a programmatic .click() never reaches its handler. There is no rollback: the composition lived in the page and the next navigation destroyed it with the text.

Rule

For buttons that act on the world (send, publish, pay) click only with a real CDP click on the element from a snapshot, the same way fill that does not stick is replaced by type_text for inputs. Confirm by the state of the world (a Message sent toast, the message visible in a folder), not by the call not throwing.
