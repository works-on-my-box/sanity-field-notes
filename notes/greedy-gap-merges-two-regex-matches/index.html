A greedy gap in a verb...noun regex merges two matches; a negation kills both

2026-09-06 · Node.js#regex

Symptom

I would not book a meeting, but let's schedule a call on Monday passed a stop rule even after switching to matchAll: book\b[^.]{0,40}\b(call|meeting) captured book a meeting, but let's schedule a call as one match, and the not before book cancelled everything.

Cause

The gap [^.]{0,40} is greedy: it stretches to the last noun within 40 characters, so a second match cannot start inside it.

Rule

In rules of the form 'verb ... noun' make the gap lazy ({0,N}?), iterate over all matches, and keep a 'refusal + promise in one sentence' case in the regression.
