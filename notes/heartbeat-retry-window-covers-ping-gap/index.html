A heartbeat's retry window must cover the gap to the next ping

2026-08-27 · Linux services#heartbeat#retries#monitoring#false-alarm

Symptom

The external health check reported DOWN while the daemon was fully alive (polls 9/9, NRestarts=0): the ping after one poll hit a 10 s timeout twice, and the next chance came only after the next poll 16 minutes later. Fourth such case in six days.

Cause

Window arithmetic: the check expects a ping every 20 min plus 10 min grace = 30 min; pings come every 15-17 min, so one missed ping is always an alarm. The single retry after 5 s covered a hiccup of up to 15 s; the real one lasted 25 s or more. The alarm measured one 25-second stretch of network, not the daemon.

Rule

Make the heartbeat retry window at least the interval between pings (here up to 10 attempts one minute apart); log the outcome of every ping and a success-after-retry as its own line, otherwise stutters are invisible and an empty grep lies. Before wiring any external check compute (period + grace) - signal interval = how many seconds of hiccup it forgives.
