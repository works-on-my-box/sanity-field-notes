Field notes — symptom → cause → rule, from an unattended browser-automation stack

RLS probes with WHERE/RETURNING test the SELECT policy, not the write policy

2026-09-30 · PostgreSQL#rls#security

Symptom

After a 'verified' fix, an independent reviewer found within half an hour that a project member could rewrite created_by on someone else's task and delete it through an author deletes policy (or move it away by changing project_id). A second reviewer showed that the two main test probes and CI stayed green with the write policies reopened: four of nine single-policy regressions were not caught.

Cause

WITH CHECK sees only the new row and cannot forbid changing an owner column; probes like update ... where ... returning and insert ... returning are stopped by the SELECT policy before the write policy is ever reached; the test was written against one known attack rather than against each policy.

Rule

For an access-control fix: (1) owner columns are locked by column privileges or a trigger, not by a policy; (2) every write is probed twice, as the app sends it and bare (without WHERE and RETURNING); (3) one probe per policy; (4) acceptance is mutational: every single weakening of a policy or grant must turn CI red; (5) after each fix, a fresh independent break-it round on a live stand before publishing.
