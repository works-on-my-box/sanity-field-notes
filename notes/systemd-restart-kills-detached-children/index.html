Field notes — symptom → cause → rule, from an unattended browser-automation stack

systemctl restart kills the whole cgroup, detached children included

2026-09-13 · Linux services#systemd#restart

Symptom

The restart rule said 'between polls: last log line is sleep NNNs and the lock is free'. But the monitor spawns the drafter with spawn(..., { detached: true }), the drafter spawns the auto-submit the same way, and systemctl show -p KillMode is control-group for both units.

Cause

Restarting a unit kills all processes of its cgroup, and detached does not help. The rule was written for the poll itself; after a poll the drafter lives 1-2 more minutes and the auto-submit with its verifier up to 5, mostly without holding the lock.

Rule

Before systemctl restart of a long-lived daemon, its cgroup must be empty: cat /sys/fs/cgroup$(systemctl show -p ControlGroup --value <unit>)/cgroup.procs shows only MainPID. For the monitor additionally require the sleep between polls. Automation that restarts the daemon must check the same thing.
