An access-control fix needs probes for what must keep working

2026-09-30 · PostgreSQL#rls#testing#ci#regression#security

Symptom

Two review rounds and a fact-check missed that after the fix the owner of a new project could not see their project or insert their first membership row (insert ... returning and the insert into project_members failed). Fresh reviewers found it by asking 'can the owner still...?', confirmed on PostgreSQL 16.14.

Cause

The post-fix test repeated only the attacker's actions, all of which were supposed to fail and did. What was supposed to keep working was not tested; the fact-check compared the article with the scripts rather than the scripts with common sense.

Rule

Any access-control fix gets two probe sets: what must stop working and what must keep working (create an object, add the first member, read your own rows); both in the test and in CI. Give an expert reviewer a temporary cluster and the task 'break the fix: a leak or a refusal of a normal action', not just the text.
